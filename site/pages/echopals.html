Echo Pals

Twelve talking animal friends for toddlers. Your child talks, and the pet says it back in a funny voice. No ads, ever.

Coming soon to iPhone and iPad

What your child can do

  • Twelve friends, each in their own world: a cat, a dog, a monkey, a duckling, a piglet, a bunny, a lion cub, a bear cub, a parrot, a baby elephant, a giraffe and a penguin.
  • Talk, tickle, feed, dance and play ball. No reading needed, made for little hands.
  • Pay once, and that's it: no ads, no subscriptions, nothing to buy inside, and it works offline.

  • Talk

    The pet perks up its ears, listens, then says it back in a squeaky, funny voice.

  • Tap and tickle

    Pat its head, stroke it, or tickle its tummy for giggles and wiggles.

  • Feed

    Each pet has a favourite snack, from fish and bones to bananas and acacia leaves. It munches, then says "Yummy!"

  • Dance

    Press the music button and the pet dances to a tune.

  • Play ball

    Something different every time: a header, a catch, a bounce, or a funny miss. Each pet plays in its own scene, with little surprises to find.

  • Nap time

    Leave it be and it yawns, looks around, and drifts off with floating Zzz.

Made for peace of mind

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and nothing to buy inside the app.
  • Works offline. The app sends nothing over the internet.
  • The microphone is used only while the app is open and listening is switched on. The sound stays in the device's memory for a few seconds and is then thrown away.
  • Settings and links sit in a grown-ups' corner behind a small sum that toddlers can't solve.
