Echo Pals

A talking pet for toddlers. Your child talks, and the pet says it back in a funny voice.

Coming soon to iPhone and iPad

What your child can do

  • Talk

    The pet perks up its ears, listens, then says it back in a squeaky, funny voice.

  • Tap and tickle

    Pat its head, stroke it, or tickle its tummy for giggles and wiggles.

  • Feed

    Fish, a bone or a banana: the pet munches, then says "Yummy!"

  • Dance

    Press the music button and the pet dances to a tune.

  • Play ball

    Something different every time: a header, a catch, a bounce, or a funny miss.

  • Nap time

    Leave it be and it yawns, looks around, and drifts off with floating Zzz.

Made for peace of mind

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and nothing to buy inside the app.
  • Works offline. The app sends nothing over the internet.
  • The microphone is used only while the app is open. The sound stays in the device's memory for a few seconds and is then thrown away.
  • Settings and links sit in a grown-ups' corner behind a small sum that toddlers can't solve.
