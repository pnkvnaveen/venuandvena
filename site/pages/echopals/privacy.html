Echo Pals

Privacy policy

Echo Pals collects no personal data. Nothing your child says or does in the app leaves the device.

Effective: 25 November 2026

Who we are

Echo Pals is made by a parent who publishes children's apps and videos as Venu & Vena. In this policy, "we" means Venu & Vena.

What Echo Pals collects

Nothing. We do not collect, store on our side, share or sell any information about you, your child or your device. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we never ask for a name, age, email address or location.

The microphone

Echo Pals asks for permission to use the microphone so the pet can hear your child and say it back in a funny voice.

  • The microphone is used only while the app is open on screen and listening is switched on.
  • What it hears stays in the device's memory for a few seconds, is played back, and is then thrown away.
  • Sound is never saved to the device, never recorded to a file and never sent anywhere.
  • You can switch listening off in the grown-ups' corner, or turn off microphone access at any time in Settings > Echo Pals. The pets still play, eat and dance without it.

What is kept on the device

The app remembers a few settings on the device only: whether sound and listening are on, whether the welcome screen has been seen, and the last pet played with. These never leave the device, and deleting the app deletes them.

The internet

Echo Pals works fully offline. Every picture, voice and sound is inside the app. The app sends nothing over the internet.

The website version

Echo Pals can also be played in a web browser. It works the same way: what the microphone hears stays on the device and is thrown away, and the few settings above are saved only in that browser on that device. It loads only its own files and contacts no other service. Cloudflare, which hosts it, keeps the standard request logs every website has (such as the IP address, browser type and time of each request) for a short time to keep the service running and safe. We do not use them to identify anyone, and there are no cookies, ads or analytics.

Ads, analytics and third parties

Echo Pals has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data. We share nothing with anyone because we have nothing to share. The pets' voices were recorded in advance and are part of the app; no voice service is contacted while your child plays.

Purchases

Echo Pals is bought once from the App Store. Apple handles the payment under Apple's own privacy policy; we never see your payment details. There is nothing to buy inside the app.

Information from Apple

If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and download totals. These do not identify you or your child, and we use them only to fix problems.

Links out of the app

The only links sit in the grown-ups' corner, behind a sum that young children cannot solve. They open this privacy policy and our help page as a web page inside the app, which you close with Done. These two pages link only to each other and to our email address.

Children's privacy

Echo Pals is made for children aged 5 and under and is listed in the App Store's Kids category. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we comply with children's privacy laws including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

If you email us

If you write to hello@venuandvena.com, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we delete them when the conversation is finished. Please don't include your child's personal details.

Changes to this policy

If Echo Pals ever changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page before the change reaches the app and change the date above.
