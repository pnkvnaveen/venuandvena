Echo Pals

Privacy policy

Echo Pals collects no data at all.

This is the short version. The full policy will be published at this same address before Echo Pals is released.

The microphone

Echo Pals uses the microphone only while the app is open, so the pet can say back what it hears. The sound stays in the device's memory for a few seconds and is then thrown away. It is never saved and never sent anywhere.

What the app keeps

The only things kept on the device are the choices made in the grown-ups' corner and the last pet played with. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app never does

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
  • Nothing to buy inside the app.
  • It sends nothing over the internet. Everything it needs is inside it.

Links out of the app

Links to this website sit in the grown-ups' corner, behind a small sum. They open your web browser, outside the app.
