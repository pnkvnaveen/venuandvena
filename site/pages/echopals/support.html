Keep your child in the app (Guided Access)

Guided Access is built into iPhone and iPad. It keeps little fingers inside Echo Pals and turns off the Home gesture.

  1. Open Settings, then Accessibility, then Guided Access, and turn it on.
  2. Choose a passcode.
  3. Open Echo Pals and quickly press the side button (or the Home button) three times, then tap Start.
  4. To finish, press it three times again and type your passcode.

Common questions

The pet doesn't say back what my child says

Echo Pals needs the microphone to hear. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Microphone, and turn on Echo Pals. Also check that listening is on in the grown-ups' corner.

How do I open the grown-ups' corner?

Press and hold the small gear at the top left (a tap only shows a hint), then type the answer to the sum. There you can turn sounds and listening on or off, and find help and privacy.

Does Echo Pals need the internet?

No. Everything it needs is inside the app, and it works offline.

Is there anything to buy, or any ads?

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy inside the app.

Is my child's voice recorded or saved?

No. The sound stays in the device's memory for a few seconds so the pet can say it back, then it is thrown away. It is never saved and never sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.
