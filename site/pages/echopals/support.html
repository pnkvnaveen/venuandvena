Keep your child in the app (Guided Access)

Guided Access is built into iPhone and iPad. It keeps little fingers inside Echo Pals and turns off the Home gesture.

  1. Open Settings, then Accessibility, then Guided Access, and turn it on.
  2. Choose a passcode.
  3. Open Echo Pals and quickly press the side button (or the Home button) three times, then tap Start.
  4. To finish, press it three times again and type your passcode.

Common questions

The pet doesn't say back what my child says
  • Check that listening is on: hold the gear at the top left for a moment, answer the sum, and look at Listening in the grown-ups' corner.
  • Check that Echo Pals may use the microphone: Settings > Echo Pals > Microphone.
  • Speak close to the device in a quiet room. The pets try to ignore TV and background noise, so very soft speech can be missed.
  • Wait until the pet has finished talking. It listens again straight after.
There's no sound

Turn the volume up with the side buttons, and check that sound is on in the grown-ups' corner. If headphones or a speaker are connected, the sound goes there.

How do I open the grown-ups' corner?

Press and hold the gear at the top left for about two seconds, then type the answer to the sum. Toddlers can't open it by tapping.

Does Echo Pals need the internet?

No. Everything is inside the app, so it works in airplane mode, on a plane or in the car.

Is there anything to buy, or any ads?

No. You pay once in the App Store and get every pet, now and in future updates. No ads, no subscriptions, nothing to buy inside the app.

Is my child's voice recorded or saved?

No. Sound stays in the device's memory for a few seconds so the pet can say it back, and is then thrown away. It is never saved or sent anywhere. See our privacy policy.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or later. It also runs on Apple silicon Macs and Apple Vision Pro as an iPad app.

I have a new phone, or I deleted the app. Do I have to pay again?

No. Open the App Store with the same Apple Account, search for Echo Pals and download it again for free. With Family Sharing turned on, everyone in your family group can download it too.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose Echo Pals.

Can I suggest a new pet or report a problem?
