About us

Calm, ad-free things for little ones, made by a parent.

Venu & Vena started with a simple wish: short, gentle videos that teach one small thing at a time, and apps that let a young child play without ads, tracking or anything pulling them to keep tapping. We couldn't find quite enough of that, so we started making it.

Meet Venu and Vena

Venu is tall, orange and full of beans. He rushes in, guesses with all his heart, and laughs when he gets it wrong.

Vena is small, teal and thoughtful, with her notebook always ready. She notices things, and helps Venu notice them too.

Together they explore a garden made of paper and find something new every time. Their names are said Vay-nu and Vay-naa.

How we make each episode

  • We start from something a young child already knows, like an apple, a ball or a puppy.
  • We show the new thing first, and only then ask about it.
  • We never use words that expect a child to know what the episode is teaching.
  • One idea per episode, kept short, with calm music and friendly voices.
  • Every episode is checked through a 3 to 5 year old's eyes before it goes out.

Our promises to parents

  • Our apps have no ads and no tracking, and never collect anything from children.
  • This website sets no cookies and has no analytics.
  • Our videos are marked as made for kids on YouTube.
  • We make things to enjoy together, with a grown-up close by.
