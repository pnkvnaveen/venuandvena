Privacy on this website

No cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

This page is about venuandvena.com. Our apps have their own policies, such as the Echo Pals privacy policy.

The short version

  • We set no cookies and use no analytics, ads or trackers.
  • Every picture, font and script comes from our own site. Nothing loads from anywhere else until you tap play on a video.
  • We collect nothing from children. This site is for parents.

Hosting

The site runs on Cloudflare. Like every website, it needs some technical details to send you a page, such as your device's IP address and browser type. We have turned off request logging for this site, and we never use these details to identify or follow anyone. Cloudflare's own handling is described in the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Videos

Video pictures come from our own site. When you tap play, the video loads from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and YouTube's privacy policy applies to that video from then on. Our videos are marked as made for kids, which limits what YouTube collects while they play.

Feedback and email

If you send feedback through our form, we keep only what you type and the date, for 12 months, and then delete it. If you give us your email address there, or email us, we use it only to reply to you. We never share or sell anything you send us.

Links to other sites

Links to YouTube, Instagram and the App Store take you to those sites, and their own privacy policies apply there.
