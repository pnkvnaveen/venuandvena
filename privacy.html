Privacy policy

Venu & Vena Studio · last updated 23 October 2026

Venu & Vena Studio ("the app") is the production tool of the Venu & Vena YouTube channel. Only the channel's owner uses it, with the owner's own Google account. This page explains what the app does with Google user data.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (scope youtube.force-ssl): the app confirms which channel the owner signed in with, uploads the owner's videos to that channel as private, and sets their thumbnail, captions and playlist. It reads back only those videos' details to check that each upload is private and correct.
  • Google Drive (scope drive.file): the app can see only the folders and files it creates itself. It uses them to keep a copy of each finished episode. It cannot see anything else in the Drive.

What the app keeps

The app keeps a small record of each upload (the video's ID and how far the upload got) so an interrupted upload can continue instead of starting again. It keeps the sign-in credentials only in the owner's private computing environment, never in code or public files. It does not collect, store or share data about viewers, and it collects nothing from children.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in their Google Account, under Security, Third-party connections. Files the app saved in Drive stay there until the owner deletes them.
