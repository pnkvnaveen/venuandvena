Venu & Vena Studio

Venu & Vena is a YouTube channel of short learning videos for children aged 3 to 5: letters, numbers and everyday discoveries, told by two friendly characters.

What this app is

Venu & Vena Studio is the channel's own production tool. It is used only by the channel's owner, on the channel's own accounts. It does two things with Google services:

  • uploads finished episodes to the Venu & Vena YouTube channel as private videos, with their thumbnail, captions and playlist, so the owner can review them before publishing them in YouTube Studio;
  • saves a copy of each finished episode in a folder it creates in the owner's Google Drive.

It is not offered to other people, it has no sign-up, and it shows no ads.
